# After a Super Memoria command TIME runs again (the interrupt is back on): WAIT 20 measures 20-24 cs
wait 1.5
type *FORMAT\n
waitfor 1 SUPER MEMORIA: INTRO 5
cart sm
press INTRO
waitfor 0 Done. 5
cart rom
press INTRO
wait 1.2
type T%=TIME:WAIT 20:PRINT (TIME-T%) DIV 5\n
wait 0.8
expectscr 2          4
quit
